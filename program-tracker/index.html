Hedera — Confirmed open

Project saysDevelopers with Hedera and smart-contract experience; or community professionals in marketing, education, UX/UI or project management.
You doWorkshops, technical content, developer support, events, community engagement, partnerships or content.
DeadlineNo deadline published.
Reward and conditionsCertificates, swag and badges. Cash or token amount not stated.
SelectionNot stated.
Official routehttps://hedera.com/ambassador-program/
People reportNo recent independent report found.
Safety or missing informationOpen the form only from Hedera’s official page. No crypto payment request was found on the page.
Last checked12 Sep 2026

Cyfrin — Confirmed open

Project says18+ people interested in blockchain education, security and community building; prior experience is suggested, not required.
You doHost events, share educational content, support newcomers and connect academic communities.
DeadlineNo deadline published.
Reward and conditionsMentorship, experience, recognition, resources and merchandise. Cash pay not stated.
SelectionApplication, then a possible video interview. The site says a response in 5–7 business days.
Official routehttps://ambassadors.cyfrin.io/
People reportNo recent independent report found.
Safety or missing informationUse the Typeform only via Cyfrin’s official page. SSCD+ certification is required within six weeks after onboarding to continue.
Last checked12 Sep 2026

Nexo — Confirmed open

Project saysExperienced people with local crypto and marketing contacts, public-speaking confidence and B2B sales experience.
You doPartnerships, content, event representation, affiliate-led growth, media outreach and localization.
DeadlineNo deadline published.
Reward and conditionsNexo claims a competitive salary and result-based incentives; amount, targets and schedule are not stated.
SelectionThe public form asks why and how you can contribute; further process not stated.
Official routehttps://nexo.com/ambassadors
People reportNo recent independent report found.
Safety or missing informationThe form requests phone and LinkedIn information. Read privacy terms and use the official Nexo page; no crypto payment request was found on the page.
Last checked12 Sep 2026

Soneium — Not confirmed

Project saysCommunity contributors, especially people with a history in Soneium; focus regions include the US, Japan, Korea and China.
You doOnboarding, community support, meetups, app guidance and builder support.
DeadlineNo deadline published.
Reward and conditionsRecognition, resources, approved activation support and possible merch or community rewards; no cash amount stated.
SelectionThe June announcement says applications are reviewed on background, history, community experience, location, availability and fit.
Official routehttps://soneium.org/en/blog/soneium-ambassador-program/
People reportNo recent independent report found.
Safety or missing informationThe official announcement links a hosted form, but its current acceptance and fields could not be verified. Do not disclose data until you verify it from the official page.
Last checked12 Sep 2026

Solana University Ambassador Program — Closed

Project says18+ undergraduates graduating after Dec 2026, available 4–6 hours weekly, and not formal ambassadors for a competing chain or company.
You doFour workshops per semester, weekly build nights and a demo day.
DeadlinePublished close: 1 Sep 2026. Passed.
Reward and conditionsSemester stipend in USDC, paid in milestone-based installments; amount not stated.
SelectionTwo 60-second videos and short answers. The page says it reviews every application.
Official routehttps://solana.com/university
People reportNo recent independent report found.
Safety or missing informationThe page still displays fields, but the stated close date has passed. Do not assume a visible form accepts submissions.
Last checked12 Sep 2026

PIVX — Referral-only

Project saysContributors with content, outreach, events or community skills who have a referral.
You doAdvocacy, content, outreach, events, mentoring and local or global initiatives across program levels.
DeadlineNo deadline published.
Reward and conditionsNot stated on the inspected program page.
SelectionPositions are referral-only; applicants are asked to state who referred them.
Official routehttps://pivx.org/ambassador-program
People reportNo recent independent report found.
Safety or missing informationThis is not a general open application. Enter through the official PIVX page and Discord route only after confirming the referrer rule.
