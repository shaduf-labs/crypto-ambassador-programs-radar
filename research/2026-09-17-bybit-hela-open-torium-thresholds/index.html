Research report · 17 Sep 2026

Two application routes added; Torium’s first $100 threshold is 50K views

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs.

Question revision 1: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Bybit’s official page adds a rolling campus application for eligible students in Ghana, South Africa, Kenya, Uganda, Ethiopia and Nigeria. HeLa adds a global application for Developer and Community Ambassador tracks. Neither offers a decision-ready individual reward. A Torium recheck makes the price of its published USDT reward visible: $100 requires 50K verified views on one public post.

Decision: Add Bybit and HeLa to the application table. Keep Pendulum separate as Discord enrollment because it publishes no application review and says the role is unpaid. Keep Wager Predict out of the confirmed table: its issuer post says apply, but its reward and regional-priority sections still contain drafting placeholders and it says mainnet awaits a third-party audit.

Routes rechecked

Hedera, Cyfrin, Soneium, Elk, Nomis, Nexo, Oasis, Fiber and Torium still show issuer-linked routes. Bitget’s Trading Builder recruitment still ends 30 Sep 2026. Solana still shows application fields despite its published passed deadline; PIVX remains referral-only and SideShift remains closed. Aster’s latest substantive issuer check remains 14 Sep because its announcement did not render again.

Safety update

Do not install a file received from a recruiter or community contact just because it uses an ambassador or Web3 job label. A Recorded Future report describes criminal campaigns that used purported ambassador, marketer and Web3-developer recruiting offers to direct people toward downloads. This is general risk context, not a finding about any listed project.
