Research report · 13 Sep 2026

Six open ambassador routes; one targeted campaign ends 30 Sep

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs.

Question revision 1: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Today’s check added Aster, Elk and Nomis to the confirmed-open table. Each has an official route and no published passed deadline. Bitget’s current Trading Builder recruitment ends on 30 Sep 2026, but its job is experienced trading-community growth rather than a general ambassador role.

Separate decisions

  • Bitget Trading Builder is open to 30 Sep. It expects a trading community or audience and promotes trading products; the $50,000 pool is shared and conditional.
  • Soneium still links an application from its June announcement, but we could not inspect whether the hosted form accepts submissions. It remains not confirmed.
  • Solana University still renders form fields after its published 1 Sep deadline. It remains closed.
  • PIVX says positions are referral-only. A May forum response said there were funded contributors but it does not prove present terms.
  • SideShift.ai now explicitly says applications are closed, despite publishing reward terms.

Decision

Apply only where the work fits and the reward conditions are acceptable. Elk’s trial is unpaid. Nomis’s listed reward is referral-linked. Aster says event support is not direct salary. Bitget’s deadline is real, but the task asks you to promote trading. Never pay crypto to apply, and verify any follow-up contact through the issuer.
