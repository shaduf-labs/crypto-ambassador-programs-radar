Research report · 19 Sep 2026

Injective opens a route. Arc does not offer a general application.

Question: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Injective now joins the formal-route table. Its official page says content creators, organizers, developers and educators can apply through an issuer-linked Zealy route. It offers INJ rewards based on contribution impact, but it does not publish an individual amount, the impact measure, hours, review time or payout rules.

Program or leadWhat the issuer saysReader decision
Injective Ambassador ProgramApply Now; create threads, videos, meetups or tutorials; INJ rewards depend on impact. The page also describes Zealy quests and XP.A confirmed route, not a decision-ready pay offer. Check the Zealy fields and ask how work is measured before committing recurring output.
Arc ArchitectsArc calls this its ambassador program but says it is not application-based. Contribution earns points; some later roles use separate application, invitation or selection.Use it as an open contribution path, not a job application. Arc says its points have no monetary value and do not guarantee tokens or an airdrop.
BNB Chain MartiansThe 2025 issuer post still says Apply, but its linked Monday form returned 404. The named current program URL redirected to BNB Chain’s homepage.Not a current route. Wait for a replacement issuer route or a closure notice; do not use copied form links.

How to use this update

  1. Choose a formal route only when the work fits your existing public record or skills.
  2. Ask what specific contribution earns a reward, who reviews it, and when payment occurs.
  3. Keep participation, a role application and token-sale or referral promotion separate.
  4. Reopen the issuer page immediately before sharing information; a public announcement can outlive its route.
Do not pay crypto, share a seed phrase, or install a recruiter’s file to apply. A current issuer link does not remove basic safety checks.

Sources

  • Injective Ambassador Program — official route, contribution types and impact-based INJ reward language.
  • Arc Architects overview and terms — non-application participation and point/reward limits.
  • BNB Chain Martians announcement — historical post whose linked application form was unavailable in this check.

No independent reward-delivery evidence was found for Injective in this check. Arc and BNB conclusions describe route type and availability, not project legitimacy.
