How we verify an ambassador route

StatusMeaningWhat it does not mean
Confirmed openA current official page visibly invites an application and provides an official route.That you will be selected, paid, or receive a response.
ClosedA published deadline has passed or the issuer says the window is closed.That old form fields are a valid submission path.
Referral-onlyThe issuer requires a referrer or nomination.Open access for every reader.
Not confirmedThere is a lead or older official announcement but no enough current proof of acceptance.That the route is fake or the program never existed.

Evidence order

  1. Official project page or announced application route.
  2. Published deadline, eligibility, work, selection and reward conditions.
  3. Independent reports, clearly labelled as reports rather than proof of current terms.
  4. Public safety guidance for advance payment, impersonation and wallet risks.

We keep a last substantive check beside every status. Source pages can change after that date.
