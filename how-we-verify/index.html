How we verify a route

StatusMeaningWhat it does not mean
Confirmed openAn issuer page or official company announcement explicitly invites an application and gives a route, with no published passed deadline.That the hosted form will accept a submission, that you will be selected, paid or get a response.
Ongoing: contribution-basedAn issuer invites people to participate and earn recognition or points, but does not offer a general application.That registration or participation guarantees a role, cash, tokens or an airdrop.
Open, referral-ledA route is open, but the stated reward mainly depends on referrals or attributed activity.A general salary or stipend.
Open: trading-builder campaignA live, dated recruitment drive whose job differs from a general community ambassador role.A suitable entry point for every contributor.
Not confirmedA visible route conflicts with a newer official programme update, or the current application or funding state is not established.That a form, title or earlier announcement is a current offer.
ClosedThe issuer says applications are closed or a published deadline passed.That old fields are a valid submission route.
Referral-onlyThe issuer says a referrer or nomination is required.Open access for every reader.
Not confirmedAn official programme may exist, but its latest public route is missing or its last stated form status was closed without a later reopening notice.That the programme is fake or never existed.

Evidence order

  1. Official project page or official company announcement.
  2. Published deadline, eligibility, work, selection and reward terms.
  3. Participant reports, labelled as reports rather than current terms.
  4. Public safety guidance for payment, impersonation and wallet risks.

Every current-status claim has a last substantive check. Source pages can change after that date. On 24 Sep 2026, Sogo’s issuer page visibly stated that applications were open, but it also required account-linked Tier 2 identity verification and described referral commission rather than a wage. A route is not decision-ready until the issuer publishes the current action and terms.
