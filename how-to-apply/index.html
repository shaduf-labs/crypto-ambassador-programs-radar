How to apply without guessing

  1. Start at the issuer’s official page. Do not use a form sent in a private message. MiCA, Autheo and the other listed programs visibly link an application route from their program page.
  2. Check the route type. MiCA pays only after an approved event is delivered; Autheo states a monthly builder commitment with no reward. Stellar has a trial, Midnight is volunteer work, and Thrilld leaves KPI compensation open. These are different commitments.
  3. Record the deadline and last substantive check. A visible form after a deadline is not an open application.
  4. Match your examples to the work. Use a shipped project, event, guide, community record, partnership or trading-community experience.
  5. Write down the reward type and its conditions. “Incentives,” grant pathways or rank promotion are not a number or a guarantee. A proposed event budget is not personal compensation. Torium’s first published direct USDT threshold is 50K verified views for $100.
  6. Read every data field before submitting. Phone, LinkedIn, student status, social profiles, wallet and identity requests all have a cost. Sogo requires Tier 2 KYC via BVN or NIN before its ambassador application; decide whether a referral-commission role warrants that disclosure.
  7. Never send crypto to unlock a role, task or payout. Do not install a file sent by a recruiter or community contact unless you independently verify it from the issuer’s site.

Referral-only is not open access. PIVX still says it needs a referral, but its programme administrator says the current budget has no funds allocated for payments. Nomis pays referral-linked fees. Confirm that a programme is actually onboarding before treating a form as an offer. HPP’s Party Knights lead has no published approval or join route yet; do not start tracked work while that is missing.
