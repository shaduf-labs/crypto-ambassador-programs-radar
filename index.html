🗂️Research updates

Last substantive check: 17 Sep 2026

Two new application routes. Torium’s $100 starts at 50K verified views.

Twelve listed ambassador routes have issuer-linked ways to apply and no published passed deadline, with the last check shown in every row. Bybit opens a country-limited campus path; HeLa advertises a global six-month program. Neither states a decision-ready individual reward. Aster remains based on its 14 Sep issuer check because the announcement did not render again.

What changed: Added Bybit Africa Campus Ambassadors for eligible students in six countries and HeLa for global developer or community contributors. Torium now makes its published payout thresholds clear: $100 starts at 50K verified views on one post. Pendulum is separate because it offers Discord enrollment, not a published application review. Wager Predict is held out because its issuer post still has unfinished reward and region terms.
