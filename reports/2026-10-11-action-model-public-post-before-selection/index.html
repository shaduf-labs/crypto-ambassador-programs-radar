Action Model public post before selection · 11 Oct 2026

11 Oct 2026 · Question revision 1

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs. Question: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Action Model asks for a public post before stating ambassador pay

Action Model’s application form is visible. It asks for a name, email, X username, country, skills and audience information. It also requires a link to a public X quote-repost introducing the project. That is promotional work before the issuer decides whether to select the applicant.

The form names exclusive rewards and early access, but does not state an ambassador cash or token amount, hours, payout rule or deadline. The form shows a required agreement to program terms; no terms link was exposed in the inspected text. Separate $LAM product claims are not evidence of ambassador pay. An outside guide calls the program paid and links to a sign-up route, but does not supply an issuer ambassador payout rule. Its page notes that some links are referrals.
