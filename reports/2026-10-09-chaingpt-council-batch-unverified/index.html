Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs. Question: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

ChainGPT has ambassador tiers; no Council batch is verified open

ChainGPT’s official Buzz documentation describes four automatic tiers based on activity and Buzz Score. The first tier lists at least 10 smart followers; higher tiers also require 1,000 to 4,000 total followers. The separate Buzz Council is invitation-only and says formal applications open in limited batches. The page gives no current batch dates, form or closing date. We did not verify a current Council intake, so this is not an open application in the table.
