Aster route unverified; Bitget names 30 Sep as its close

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs. Question revision 1: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

20 issuer-linked routes remain in the confirmed-open table. Each row has its own Last checked date.

Aster: current application action not inspectable

The tracked Aster issuer URL returned only a loading shell on 30 Sep. It did not expose the announcement or application action in this check. Our 14 Sep check captured an issuer statement that applications were open year-round and that direct salary was not included. Search results still show that older text, but do not prove current intake. Aster is now in the not-confirmed tracker, not the open table.

Bitget: published final date, not verified submission

Bitget’s issuer article still says recruitment runs through 30 Sep 2026. It does not give a closing hour or time zone. The linked application response could not be inspected, so do not assume a submission is accepted. Applicants need more than 500 social followers or a community of at least 100 members and substantial trading experience. The $50,000 promotion pool is shared, partly first-come, first-served, and does not state what one Builder would receive.

Before sharing personal details or promoting trading products, open the route from the issuer page and confirm its current state. Do not use an unverified alternative form or treat a shared pool as personal pay.

What to check next

Recheck Bitget after 30 Sep and inspect Aster in a browser that fully renders the issuer page. Keep older row dates visible until those routes are substantively rechecked.
