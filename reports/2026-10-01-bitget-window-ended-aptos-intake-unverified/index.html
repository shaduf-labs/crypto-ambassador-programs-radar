Bitget’s published window ended; Aptos intake remains unverified

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs. Question revision 1. Exact question: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Bitget’s official Trading Builder article still gives 30 Sep 2026 as its recruitment end. Its linked activity page did not expose a readable submission state. We mark the campaign closed by its published date, not by proof that the form stopped accepting entries. A later issuer extension would change this assessment.

The Aptos Collective page has an Apply Now link, but says new members enter quarterly cohorts and directs readers to watch for application-window announcements. Its linked form did not expose acceptance state in this check. We list Aptos as not confirmed. It says there is no set monthly stipend; top Vanguard contributors may receive monthly awards, and grants support approved activity. Neither is guaranteed base pay.
