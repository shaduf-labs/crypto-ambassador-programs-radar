24 Sep 2026 · Last substantive check: 24 Sep 2026

Sogo is open. Its ID check comes before the application.

Sogo’s public ambassador page says applications are open. It also says an applicant must have a good-standing Sogo account and Tier 2 identity verification through BVN or NIN before applying.

Decision: Treat Sogo as a referral-commission route, not a salary. Do not tell anyone to trade or complete KYC until you understand that the commission depends on a qualifying transaction, a seven-day vesting period, and terms the issuer says may change.

What this changes

The Radar now separates Sogo’s live, account-linked referral offer from contributor roles and wages. A public route can be open while the real decision begins with identity data, financial-service promotion and another user’s qualifying activity.
