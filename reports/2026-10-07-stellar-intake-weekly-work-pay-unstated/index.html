Stellar intake and weekly commitment · 7 Oct 2026

7 Oct 2026 · Question revision 1

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs. Question: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Stellar links a program-wide intake; individual pay is not stated

The official Stellar enrollment guide links a program-wide Airtable form and says applicants receive an email with chapter next steps. It describes voluntary work including weekly Discord meetings, in-person events and content. The Rise In partner page says applications and selection are ongoing, proof of work is required and a trial applies in five named country chapters. These pages support an issuer-linked route, not proof that either form accepted a submission on 7 Oct.

Rise In describes recognition, possible grant pathways, top-performer event support and a creator prize pool. It does not state an individual salary, award amount or payout rule. A project grant is not automatic ambassador pay. The Hedera page also still linked its application on 7 Oct, without a stated cash amount; its hosted form acceptance could not be inspected.

Decision: If you need guaranteed pay or a defined weekly ceiling, ask for those terms before applying. If you want developer experience, compare the actual technical work with the weekly community and event commitments. We found no independent outcome report that proves selection or payment for this intake.
