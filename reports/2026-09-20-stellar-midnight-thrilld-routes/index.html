Research report · 20 Sep 2026

Three new routes. One is volunteer, two leave pay terms open.

Pool topic: Crypto ambassador programs.

Question revision 1: Which crypto ambassador programs are genuinely open now, who can apply, and what work, rewards, deadlines and risks come with each opportunity?

Stellar Ambassadors, Midnight Nightforce and the Thrilld Labs Ambassadorship each show an issuer or issuer-partner call to apply. Their labels conceal three very different bargains: Stellar requires proof of work and says a trial applies; Midnight says the work is volunteer-based; Thrilld says compensation depends on KPI-tracked achievements without publishing the KPI or amount.

Decision: Add all three to the confirmed-open table because their program pages visibly invite applications and show no passed deadline. Do not rank them as paid work. A form is an entry route, not proof of acceptance, individual reward or payment.

What is newly checked

RouteWhat the issuer saysReader decision
Stellar AmbassadorsRise In’s Stellar partner page says applications are open for people joining country-focused chapters; Rise In currently presents routes in Türkiye, India, Indonesia, Vietnam and the Philippines. It offers Builder, Catalyst and Voice tracks; selection is ongoing, proof of work is required and a trial applies.Fit depends on shown work, not a generic sign-up. It names an official title, direct access, possible funding pathways and creator rewards, but no cash amount, funding rule or award timing. The linked Google Form was not inspectable in this check.
Midnight NightforceMidnight’s official page has an Apply Now route for long-term ecosystem contributors and says the program operates through 2026. It describes outreach and community work, with strong contributors able to progress toward leadership.This is not a pay offer. Midnight explicitly calls it a volunteer foundation and says immediate financial rewards are not the point. Hours, review path and selection criteria are not stated.
Thrilld Labs AmbassadorshipThrilld’s official page invites global Web3 professionals with existing industry connections to apply. The work can include partnerships, regional representation, content, internal calls and partner events; ambassadors choose their monthly time and activities.The issuer lists non-cash benefits and compensation tied to participation and KPI-tracked achievements, without an amount, KPI or payment timing. The linked hosted form timed out in this check, so inspect it from the issuer page.

Use this update

  1. Choose the route only if you can show work it explicitly values: a shipped project, event, creator record, local community or professional network.
  2. Before recurring work, ask what is reviewed, what the trial means, what KPI applies, and what exact contribution earns a reward.
  3. Keep an official title, potential grant path, event access, volunteer rank and cash compensation separate.
  4. Open the issuer page immediately before sharing contact, social or wallet information. Do not pay crypto, share a seed phrase or install a recruiter’s file to apply.

Sources

  • Stellar Ambassadors — Rise In — issuer-partner route, country focus, tracks, ongoing selection, proof of work and trial.
  • Midnight Nightforce — official Apply Now route and volunteer, no-immediate-reward framing.
  • Thrilld Labs Ambassadorship — official application call, work, flexible time and KPI-linked compensation language.

No recent independent reward-delivery report was found for these routes. Hosted-form access did not establish submission acceptance, selection, payment or data fields.
